How We Verify Listings

Attachify's listings come directly from company career and attachment pages, not from anonymous submissions. That is the core of what "verified" means here: every opportunity you see traces back to an organization's own posting, not a third party's paraphrase of it.

Where listings come from

Each listing is sourced from a specific company or institution's own careers, attachment, or internship page. We do not accept unsolicited submissions from individuals claiming to represent a company.

Kept current

Listings are checked on a regular schedule and removed once they expire or are taken down at the source, rather than being left to accumulate indefinitely.

Check it yourself

Every opportunity page links directly to the original posting. However good our extraction is, the original is always one click away, so you never have to take our word for it.
